01 — About

A bit about me

I'm an AI Engineer and Data Scientist with a strong foundation in machine learning and deep learning, as well as hands-on experience building generative AI applications. What motivates me is the challenge of transforming complex, real-world data into intelligent systems that are reliable, practical, and capable of creating meaningful impact.

My professional background combines more than three years of experience in data analytics within the banking sector with hands-on work in data engineering and AI applications. This combination has given me a strong appreciation for the entire lifecycle of data-driven systems, from data quality and infrastructure to model development and deployment.

In recent years, I have focused on artificial intelligence, working with technologies such as LLMs, retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), vector databases, and multimodal deep learning. I have contributed to the development and deployment of AI solutions in production environments while continuing to deepen my expertise through advanced academic training.

I hold a Master's degree in Data Science, Big Data & Business Analytics and have completed the coursework requirements for a Master's in Deep Learning at the Polytechnic University of Madrid (UPM), with the thesis defense currently pending. My master's thesis explores the generalization limits of multimodal GNN-GRU architectures for clinical depression assessment using the DAIC-WOZ dataset, with a particular focus on understanding the challenges that arise when deploying AI systems beyond controlled research settings.
